# 15-game benchmark descriptions

## G01 — Terminal Number Guess (simple, terminal)

Guess a hidden number from 1–100; give higher/lower hints, count valid attempts, and end on a correct guess.

## G02 — Terminal Tic-Tac-Toe (simple, terminal)

Two-player 3x3 Tic-Tac-Toe with alternating X/O turns, win detection, invalid-move handling and draw detection.

## G03 — Pygame Dodger (simple, pygame)

Move horizontally to dodge falling blocks; survival time increases score, collision ends the round, and restart begins a new round.

## G04 — Pygame Breakout (simple, pygame)

Breakout game with paddle, ball, bricks, wall/paddle collisions, score and a win state after all bricks are cleared.

## G05 — Terminal Snake (simple, terminal)

Grid-based Snake; continuous movement, food collection and growth, no direct reversal, and game over on wall/self collision.

## G06 — Pygame Gem Collector (simple, pygame)

Top-down player collects five gems; each unique gem adds one point, fifth gem wins, R restarts.

## G07 — Pygame Platformer (medium, pygame)

Side-scrolling platformer with running, jumping, gravity, platforms, coins, enemies, checkpoints and goal flag.

## G08 — Pygame Top-Down Dungeon (medium, pygame)

Dungeon crawler with four-way movement, walls, keys, locked doors, health, melee attacks, enemies and exit.

## G09 — Pygame Lane Racer (medium, pygame)

Three-lane racing game with traffic, acceleration, collision penalties, distance progression and finish line.

## G10 — Godot Tower Defense (medium, godot)

Tower-defense game with enemy waves, path-following enemies, currency, tower placement/upgrades and base health.

## G11 — Godot Inventory Puzzle (medium, godot)

Puzzle adventure with grid inventory, stackable keys, weight capacity, switches, linked doors and escape-room exit.

## G12 — Pygame Wave Survival (medium, pygame)

Top-down arena survival: movement, attacks, pursuing enemies, experience, level-up choice UI, timed waves and game over.

## G13 — Godot Boss Arena (complex, godot)

Boss arena with stamina, dodge invulnerability frames, three boss phases, telegraphed attacks, stagger windows, healing charges and terminal states.

## G14 — Godot Stealth Infiltration (complex, godot)

Stealth game with patrols, vision cones, occlusion, noise propagation, suspicion, hiding spots, keycard doors, alarms and extraction.

## G15 — Godot Tactical Roguelite (complex, godot)

Turn-based tactical roguelite with squad action points, cover, line of sight, status effects, enemy turns, loot, permadeath and multi-room progression.
