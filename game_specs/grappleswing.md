Create a one-screen physics game called “Grapple Swing” where a small ball hangs from a fixed anchor point at the top of the screen by a rope and swings back and forth under gravity like a pendulum. Pressing Space while the ball is swinging releases the rope, letting the ball fly off in the direction of its current momentum as a projectile under gravity. The goal is to release at the right moment so the ball lands inside a target zone drawn near the bottom of the screen; landing inside it adds 1 point and resets the ball to the anchor to swing again. Landing outside the target zone or hitting the bottom edge also resets the ball with no point. Generate exactly one small image asset: the ball. Draw the rope as a simple line, the anchor as a small solid-colour dot, and the target zone as a plain coloured rectangle — no other images. Show the score in the top-left corner at all times. Press R to restart with score 0 and the ball back at the anchor. Press Escape to quit. Print a line to the console every time a control takes effect: RELEASE, RESTART and QUIT, and print SCORE when the ball lands in the target zone. No sounds. Keep it to as few files as possible.
